Entry for the make.fun bounty
[A Day of One-Line Pitches](https://make.fun/bounty/u-ronigrv-a-day-of-one-line-pitches-12cd9bb3):
one sentence, at most 240 characters, that would convince a friend who has
never coded to let an AI agent build their first app idea on make.fun.

## The pitch

> Tell it the app you've been picturing in your head, in your own words, and
> by tonight you'll be tapping around a real first version of it — no code, no
> lessons, just your idea finally working.

192 characters, one sentence. It gives the friend a concrete reason to say yes
now (a real first version to tap around by tonight), makes handing over the
idea feel easy and low risk (plain words, no code, no lessons), and promises
nothing more than a first version — no overpromising.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts   # Node 22; writes the pitch to /output/entry.txt
```
